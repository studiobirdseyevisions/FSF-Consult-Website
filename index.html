UNICAF UNIVERSITY · ZAMBIA

A flexible way to move your future forward.

UNICAF University in Zambia offers bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral programmes online or on campus, with student support in Lusaka. It brings recognised higher education within reach while giving you more flexibility in how you study.

Eligible students may qualify for a Unicaf Scholarship. Ask FSF to help you explore your options and register your interest through us. Scholarship awards and eligibility are assessed individually.
