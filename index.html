Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

Ask a question↑
Can I study fully online from Ethiopia?

Yes, for most courses. 39 of the 41 programmes are taught online. Nursing and Medicine are taught on campus in Lusaka, Zambia.

What language are the courses taught in?

Courses are taught in English. You can talk to FSF in Amharic or English.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and on any scholarship you receive. Ask FSF for the current fees for the course you're interested in.

Can I get a scholarship?

Yes, if you're eligible. Through FSF's partnership with Unicaf University, you can get a scholarship of up to 82% of your tuition. Awards are assessed individually, and FSF can help you apply.

When can I start?

Soon. Unicaf University has a new start date every month, so you don't have to wait for one intake a year. Tell us when you'd like to begin and we'll help you get ready in time.

What do I need to apply?

For a bachelor's: a full Grade 12 certificate with 5 O-level credits including English, or the equivalent from your country. For a master's: an accredited bachelor's degree with a Credit or better. Doctoral study needs a master's degree, a research proposal and two recommendation letters. Each course page lists its exact requirements, and FSF can check your certificates.
