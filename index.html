Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

Ask a question↑
Can I study fully online from Ethiopia?

Yes, for most courses. 39 of the 41 programmes are taught online. Nursing and Medicine are taught on campus in Lusaka, Zambia.

What language are the courses taught in?

Courses are taught in English. You can talk to FSF in Amharic or English.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and on any scholarship you receive. Ask FSF for the current fees for the course you're interested in.

Can I get a scholarship?

Eligible students may qualify for a Unicaf Scholarship. Awards and eligibility are assessed individually, and FSF can help you register your interest.

What do I need to apply?

It depends on the level and the programme. A bachelor's degree usually needs a secondary school certificate, and a master's usually needs a bachelor's degree. Tell us your background and we'll check for you.
